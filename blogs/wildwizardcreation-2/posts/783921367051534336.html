wildwizardcreation answered:

I think you're right on the money with both takes. Grian has repeatedly shown he takes his projects more seriously than they're supposed to be and he has a specific issue with taking criticism far too personally.

Which on one hand is understandable. He's an artist and this is his art, to an extent. But on the other hand there's a very real distinction between a handful of people on reddit calling him and his series shitty vs a large portion of the supportive fandom begging for a return to simplicity and the life series' core idea: unfamiliar RP between creators they're familiar with. The fact that this death game mechanic was so stupid simple was the reason it was so easy for fans to pick it up and take off with it. And fan interaction, enjoyment, and spreading of the content is the crux of any good rp based minecraft series nowadays, which *is* what the life series is whether grian wants to admit it or not.

This is honestly also something Scar has an issue with too but the difference is confusion/miscommunication on where "criticism" may be coming from. Like that tweet abt "old scar" pushing himself too hard to finish as many projects as fast as possible where the op was saying how much they're enjoying the laid-back vibe of season 10, but Scar took it as an "I miss the old scar" sentiment. Or the people on reddit saying "oh scarian is so forced oh i hate when scar and grian are duoed" because those weren't criticisms towards their friendship or them as people, those were (fair but probably poorly worded) criticisms of how the fandom treats them as a pair.

I think the dissonance between jumping the shark vs the never-ending fight to continually impress your audience with New Stuff is the root of the life series problem and will eventually be the reason for it's ironic and tragic death. I wish there was a way to get the idea that, no actually, the fandom *likes* and *wants* the simplicity of the first 2 life series seasons back *because* it was such a fresh new concept. It was easily digestible for viewers and approachable from an entertainment standpoint for the creators/participants, both of which are flat out not true anymore.

And i also think mumbo should get grian pregnant by hitting him with a bus. That's also a very good point.
