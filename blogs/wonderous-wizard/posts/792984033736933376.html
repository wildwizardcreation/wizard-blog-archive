btw this is a much shorter version of something i'll end up saying on my site but i do think it's important to mention:

i don't stick around because i "thrive off of attention" or whatever. i used to exist heavily in discourse spaces for that exact reason! nowadays i stay to myself and my group of friends and i'm much better off because of it. i don't keep going through the chore of remaking an entire blog, blocking a list of people i know are uncomfortable with my work, and adding the new blog to the list of inevitably dead social media with my name attached, just because i like being yelled at. i do it because i love the friends i've made in this community and the people i've met through my work. every time i have to go on hiatus i get so miserable because i want to talk to people! i want to see the art and headcanons and clips and aus and everything wonderful this community makes constantly!! i've only ever considered crossing tumblr off of my socials list once, after my 5th or 6th suspension, but that plan was quickly put to bed when i realized that would mean leaving behind one of my favorite fandom spaces i've ever been a part of.

tldr i stick around/keep remaking because i love making art and sharing it with this crazy fun community full of so many funny, kind, creative, amazing people :] ty all for being so patient and continuing to support me!!!
