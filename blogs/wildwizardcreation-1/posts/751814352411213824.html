picture with me, if you will, scar finally coming home from a major surgery that has everyone checking up on him and always offering to be on a call if he needs some company. He's looking at a few months worth of recovery time which means heavy pain meds for the first couple of weeks. The kinds that make him drowsy and loopy and have his friends all cooing at him over discord when he says something stupid. He hates it, but what he hates more is not being able to remember exactly what he said or mentioned between doses. He tries to ask but the hermits never give him straight answers. They don't want to embarrass him, or whatever happens in the LNC chat stays in the LNC chat, vague excuses that frustrate him but he knows it isn't the end of the world.

It's late about a week after he's got home, and he has grian in his earbuds while laying in bed, they aren't talking about anything in particular. How scar's feeling. How's traveling been for grian. What their future content plans are. All while grian's streaming his work on his latest project just for him. A one on one call with no chance of someone coming in uninvited. scar risks asking if he's said anything in front of everyone these past few days. his voice is all rough mumbles and sleepy sighs, muffled slightly by how he's turned to face his phone. It makes grian's stomach flip when he remembers one recently discovered fun fact about scar. a fact that was, admittedly, coerced out of him, but still.

it came up while some of the hermits were helping tear down a mountain near one of their bases. It was just 5 or 6 of them, including scar, who was sitting at his desk but not participating. his character standing idly on some clumsily placed oak stairs, watching his friends work away. the conversation started innocent enough. how's the wife? how're the kids? having kids is weird. being married is weird. Each of them taking their turns to laugh about how being grown is so strange. scar's content to sit back and listen. but it quickly devolves into reminiscing, as usual. The media they enjoyed as kids, who was rowdy and who was shy, how each of them slotted into their highschools, into their universities. Doc brings up losing his virginity first. 16, he brags, with an older girl too. They all laugh, but stories quickly start popping up. Bdubs and his glorious body count of 2 total. Etho mumbles something about a locker room, they don't pry. Cub mentions a professor at college and laughs off everyone's dramatic gasps. Grian tries to not be too obvious when he brings up fooling around during his time at university. The word boyfriend is brought up briefly, and put away even faster. Though it was becoming increasingly obvious how quiet scar had gotten. Grian comes over to punch scar's character off of his seat, checking to make sure he hadn't fallen asleep at his desk again. Scar sounds a lot more whiny than he intended when he confirmed he was awake, jumping around a bit, punching the stone beneath him, settling back on the stairs and moving his hands away from his keyboard.

etho's the one brave enough to be nosey first. 'you were a swimmer, right scar?' the man in question already moving to hide away behind his hands, cheeks burning, half coherent mind trying to formulate some lie about a dance maybe. or something behind the bleachers. after hours when the pool was closed. those all sounded cool, right? real enough? doc makes some comment about rabbits. scar shrinks down in his chair. grian's already in his DMs. he doesn't need to talk about it, he promises, he can shift the conversation if scar wants, just let him know if he's uncomfortable. there ends up being enough prying for scar to mutter out an embarrassed mention of kissing one of the members of the girls team once. maybe some heavy petting. he can't remember, it was a long time ago. it's another 30 minutes of teasing and playful flirting and condescending 'awww's whenever scar would respond to their poking fun with little pathetic whines instead of words before grian suggests he needs some rest. it was almost 11 for him. he'd need to take more meds, he wouldn't want to worry his parents, it's okay, they'll be there tomorrow. scar leaves the game with their conversation already fizzling off into half memories. grian watches scar leave with the sound of his admittance burned into his eardrums.

'i don't think you've said anything too egregious.' grian mumbles in response to scar's question. he can hear scar's breathing slowing down. he can almost picture his pretty green eyes struggling to stay open. scar sighs something that was probably meant to sound like an okay. grian crosses his legs, staring at the little square with scar's profile picture lighting up when he shifts, as if trying to will it to turn on with his mind alone. 'id tell you if you did. don't worry.'
